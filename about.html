The event

Why we're doing this

The Quantum Club @ OSU exists to make quantum computing something you can actually try rather than only read about. Qiskit Fall Fest is the biggest thing we run all year: a week where anyone at Ohio State can go from a first circuit to a finished project, with the people who've already been through it sitting right there to help. Ohio State was selected by IBM Quantum to host a Fall Fest event this year, and every part of it — the talk, the workshop, the problems, the judging — is put together by students.
