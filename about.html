Who we are

About

Qiskit Fall Fest at The Ohio State University is organized by The Quantum Club @ OSU, with support from IBM Quantum and our sponsors.

Our Mission

The Quantum Club @ OSU exists to make quantum computing accessible -- something you can actually try rather than just read about. We teach and train people in using and understanding quantum technologies. Qiskit Fall Fest is a week where anyone can go from a first circuit to a finished project, with experienced mentors available to help. Every part of the fest is organized by fellow students.
