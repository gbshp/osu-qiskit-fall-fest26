What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

Learn quantum computing, hands-on

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of student-organized events supported by IBM Quantum. At Ohio State, we're bringing together students of all backgrounds — no prior quantum experience required — for a day of workshops, talks, and a mini hackathon using Qiskit, IBM's open-source quantum computing SDK.

🧑‍🏫

Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering quantum computing fundamentals, Qiskit basics, and real quantum hardware access — beginner-friendly, no experience needed.

🎤

Guest Talks

Hear from researchers, industry professionals, and IBM Quantum team members about the state of quantum computing and where the field is headed.

💡

Mini Hackathon

Team up and build a small quantum project by the end of the day, with mentors on hand to help and prizes for standout projects.

Who should come?

Everyone is welcome

Whether you've never written a line of Qiskit or you're already running circuits on real quantum hardware, there's something here for you.

New to quantum?

Start with our intro workshop track — we'll walk through qubits, gates, and your first circuit in Qiskit, step by step.

Already coding in Qiskit?

Jump into the hackathon track, dive into advanced talks, and get access to real IBM Quantum hardware.
