What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

One week to build something quantum

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of student-organized quantum computing events supported by IBM Quantum, and this fall The Ohio State University is hosting one. We open on Friday, November 6 with a guest talk and a beginner-friendly Qiskit workshop, then release the hackathon problems. You have the following week to build — with office hours along the way — and we close on Friday, November 13 with presentations, judging, and prizes.

🎤

Guest Talk

We kick off with a talk on where quantum computing actually stands today and what people are using it for — accessible whether or not you've touched a qubit before.

🧑‍🏫

Opening Workshop

A hands-on Qiskit session right after the talk: qubits, gates, circuits, and running your first program on real IBM Quantum hardware. Start from zero, leave with working code.

💡

Hackathon

Form a team, pick a problem, and build over the week. Organizers hold office hours throughout so you're never stuck alone, and the best projects take home prizes.

Who should come?

All backgrounds, all majors

There are no tracks and no prerequisites. Physics, CS, math, engineering, business, art — we've built the week so that someone seeing a quantum circuit for the first time on November 6 can still ship a project by the 13th. If you already write Qiskit, there's plenty of room to go deep, and mentors to push you.

Never seen a qubit?

The opening workshop assumes nothing. A little Python helps, but we'll get you from "what is superposition" to a running circuit in one evening.

Already writing circuits?

Skip ahead to the hackathon problems, use office hours to talk through your approach, and get free access to real IBM Quantum backends.

Ready to join us?

Registration is free and takes a couple of minutes. Then hop in the event Discord — that's where teams form, problems drop, and questions get answered all week.
