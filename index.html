What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of student-organized quantum computing events supported by IBM Quantum. This November, The Quantum Club @ The Ohio State University will be hosting a Qiskit Fall Fest PLUS. We open on Friday, November 6 with a guest talk by IBM Quantum and a beginner-friendly Qiskit workshop. The hackathon prompts drop shortly after. You have the following week to build with office hours along the way. On Friday, November 13, we finish with presentations, judging, and prizes!

🎤

Guest Speaker

By IBM Quantum. Where does quantum computing stand today and what are people using it for? Where is the industry headed?

🧑‍🏫

Opening Workshop

A hands-on Qiskit session: qubits, gates, circuits, and running your first program on real IBM Quantum hardware. Start from zero, leave with working code.

💡

Hackathon

Form a team, pick a problem, and build over the week. Organizers hold office hours throughout so you're never stuck. The best projects win prizes!

Who should come?

All Backrounds Welcome

There are no prerequisites.Physics, CS, math, engineering and business majors are all welcome! We've built the week so that someone seeing a quantum circuit for the first time on November 6 can still ship a project by the 13th. There's also room for those experienced with Qiskit build something more advanced.

Ready to join us?

Registration is free and takes a couple of minutes. Don't forget to join the Discord, where teams are formed, problems drop, and questions get answered throughout the week.
