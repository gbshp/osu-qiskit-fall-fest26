November 6–13, 2026

Schedule

Two Friday evenings bookend a week of building. Times are close to final; rooms and office-hour slots are still being confirmed.

Friday, November 6 — Kickoff

5:00 – 6:00 PM
Check-in & Mixer — Atrium Pick up a name tag, grab food, and find teammates before we start.
6:00 – 6:45 PM
Opening Remarks & Guest Talk — Room 1080 What quantum computing can and can't do right now. Speaker to be announced. Talk
6:45 – 8:15 PM
Qiskit Workshop — Room 1080 Qubits, gates, and circuits from scratch, ending with everyone running a program on real IBM Quantum hardware. Bring a laptop. Workshop
8:15 – 8:30 PM
Hackathon Kickoff Problems released, teams registered, rules and judging criteria walked through. Hackathon

Saturday, November 7 – Thursday, November 12 — Build Week

All week
Work on your project Hack on your own schedule, wherever you like. Team channels and the problem statements live in the event Discord. Hackathon
TBD
Office Hours — Room 1080 Drop in with organizers and mentors to debug, sanity-check an idea, or get unstuck. Days and times announced in the Discord.

Friday, November 13 — Demo Night

5:00 – 6:00 PM
Final Work & Submissions — Scott Laboratory 045, 050, 054 Last push, dinner, and project submissions. Hackathon
6:00 – 8:00 PM
Project Presentations & Judging Every team presents what they built to a panel of judges. Hackathon
8:00 – 9:00 PM
Awards & Closing Prizes for standout projects, then a chance to hang around and talk shop.

Questions about the schedule? Ask in the event Discord or get in touch on the About page.
