Good to know

FAQ

Do I need quantum computing or programming experience?

No. The opening workshop starts from the very beginning, and there are no separate tracks — everyone gets the same problems and the same support. Some Python familiarity makes life easier, but plenty of people arrive without it.

What majors is this for?

All of them. Quantum computing pulls from physics, computer science, math, and engineering, but the problems reward good ideas from anywhere. If you're curious, you belong here.

Do I need a team?

No — show up solo and we'll help you find teammates at the kickoff mixer and in the Discord. You're also welcome to bring your own team.

Do I have to be there the whole week?

Only the two Friday evenings really matter: kickoff on November 6 and presentations on November 13. In between, you work on your own schedule and drop into office hours if you want them.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Yes, anything that runs a modern browser. We use IBM Quantum's cloud tools, so there's nothing to install.

Is it really free?

Yes — registration, food, materials, and quantum hardware access are all free.

Can students from other universities join?

Ohio State students come first, but students from other schools are welcome as space allows. Register early if you're travelling in.
