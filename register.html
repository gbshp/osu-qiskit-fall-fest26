Good to know

FAQ

Do I need prior quantum computing or programming experience?

No. We have a beginner-friendly workshop track that starts from the basics. Familiarity with Python is helpful but not required.

Is this only for Ohio State students?

Ohio State students are our priority, but students from other universities are welcome to join as space allows.

Do I need to bring my own laptop?

Yes — please bring a laptop that can run a web browser. All workshops use IBM Quantum's cloud-based tools, so no local installation is required.

Is there a cost to attend?

No, the event is completely free, including food and workshop materials.

Can I just come for part of the day?

Of course — feel free to drop in for the sessions that interest you most, though we'd love to have you for the whole day!
